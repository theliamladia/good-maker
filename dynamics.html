IN THE DYNAMICS APP · BUY A HOMEPOD

  1. GET ONEBuy it in the DYNAMICS app ("BUY A HOMEPOD", $25,000). Admins can give one with /cars homepod.
  2. PLACE ITRight-click the top of a block. It sits on the ground as a flat 3×3 pad with a blue light ring and the GD logo.
  3. PAIR ITRight-click the pad and pick one of your GD cars or planes (you need that vehicle's key on you). Each vehicle can have one Homepod. Sneak and right-click picks the pad back up.
  4. DOCKA DOCK button then shows on that vehicle's row in the DYNAMICS app. Press it and the vehicle goes straight back onto its pad from wherever it is, cars and planes alike.
  5. CHARGINGAn electric car parked on its pad with nobody in it charges by itself, from empty to full in about 2 minutes. The % shows above the pad, and the owner pays once at the end, at the same per-kWh price as the chargers. Planes dock there but don't charge.
