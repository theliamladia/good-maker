↓ WALK DOWN THE VALLEY

I · BEFORE DAWN

You left before the light did. The trail out of town goes quiet after the last fence post, and the valley opens up below you, still blue, still asleep. Somewhere behind the mountain, the sun is deciding.

II · FIRST LIGHT

First light hits the ridge and runs down it like water. Your jacket has been yours for years: the indigo gone soft, the elbows shaped to you. Underneath, the henley is the only thing that changes. Nothing else needs to.

TETON®

For the wandering cowboy. A lone stroll through the wilderness with nothing but your wits and the beauty of nature.

HERITAGE™ JEAN JACKET WITH HENLEY
Worn open, one indigo throughout. The henley carries the colour.
HERITAGE™ JEANS · UNIQUEⓤ
Five pockets, bootcut, worn soft by hard days on the ranch. A one-off wash, marked ⓤ.

III · THE PASS

By mid-morning the trail gives out. Weather comes over the pass without asking. You pull the hood up, cinch the hem, and keep walking. Nothing out here is going to tell you where to stop.

YELLOWSTONE®

For the explorer. No adverse condition can stop your wanderlust.

HERITAGE™ ANORAK
Half-zip pullover, hood up or down, kangaroo pocket, drawcord hem. DUCK HUNTER™ at every edge.
HERITAGE™ BAGGY CAMO TROUSER
Cut loose and stacked over the boot, in brown camo to match.

IV · CAMP

Evening. You come down to the lake where the camp is. Smoke from the fire, a canoe pulled up on the stones, somebody calling everyone in. Mud to the knee, everyone in the same blue, faded to their own shade.

CAMP®

The latest in uniform options for GOOD®. A summer camp outfit, built to get dirty.

GOOD® BLUE / MARSHMALLOW LINEN™
MARSHMALLOW™, woven loose in linen for summer, trimmed in our blue.
CAMP SHORT
Heavy canvas, rolled hem, GOOD® BLUE webbing belt, wool socks and hiking boots.

V · NIGHTFALL

The sun goes back behind the mountain. Tomorrow, you wander again.
