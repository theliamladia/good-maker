PRIVACY

Your data, plainly.

GOOD® keeps as little as it can. No ads, no tracking, no selling anything to anyone. Here is everything we store, why, and how to get rid of it. Last updated 26 October 2026.

Without an account

MAKER® works entirely in your browser. A skin you upload or fetch by username is put together on your device and never stored by us. To fetch a skin by username we ask Mojang (and, for player heads, mc-heads, Crafatar, Minotar or PlayerDB) for it, which shares that username with them.

Your browser keeps a few small notes for this site only (its local storage, not cookies): your MAKER® settings and last fit, and whether you've seen the intro and the three steps. They never leave your device; clear your browser's site data to remove them.

If you post a comment on STUDY® without signing in, we keep the name you type (optional), the comment and the date.

With a GOOD® account

  • Email address. Only to send you sign-in links. We never send anything else, and we don't share it.
  • Your GOOD® name. Shown on your comments and MARKET® listings, and it's your public profile link.
  • Public profile. good.leems.me/profiles/your-name shows your GOOD® name, when you joined, your achievements, your GOOD® CRATE pulls and serials, your GOOD® COINS, your saved fits (and the names you give them), your home skin and the comments others leave there. MEMBERS® lists your name, GOOD® COINS, achievement count, when you joined and any tag (like STREAMER). Never your email.
  • GOOD® CRATE and achievements. Every piece you pull, with its serial and date, and the achievements you've earned.
  • Home skin. The skin image you save, so MAKER® can load it for you.
  • Wardrobe. The fits you save, stored as a list of piece names, not pictures.
  • GOOD® COINS. Your balance and how many you've earned today.
  • MARKET®. Pieces you list are public while they're for sale: the piece, its serial, the price, and your GOOD® name and face as the seller. Every list, unlist, buy and sale is kept in your MY TRADES history, and a sale also appears in the buyer's or seller's history and, without names, on the public tape.
  • MAKER® settings. Your last fit and body type, so MAKER® opens the way you left it.
  • Comments. What you post on STUDY® or on someone's profile, with your GOOD® name and the date.
  • Suggestion box. What you write, with your GOOD® name and the date. Only GOOD® reads it.
  • MINECRAFT STAND. When you ask for a stand code, the finished skin is sent to MineSkin to be signed for Minecraft; we keep the code, the signed skin and the fit's name so the stand can load it.

Cookies

One cookie, only when you sign in: it keeps you signed in for 30 days. It can't be read by the page's scripts and isn't used for anything else. No analytics or advertising cookies.

Abuse protection

To stop spam, we briefly count requests per connection. The IP address is scrambled (hashed) and the count disappears after a minute.

Who helps us run it

  • Vercel hosts the site and may keep standard server logs.
  • Upstash stores accounts, wardrobes and comments.
  • Resend delivers the sign-in emails.
  • Mojang supplies skins when you look one up by username; mc-heads, Crafatar, Minotar and PlayerDB supply player heads and look up usernames.
  • MineSkin signs the skin when you make a MINECRAFT STAND code (the skin is stored with them and on Minecraft's texture servers).
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr serve the fonts and the 3D viewer, so they see your IP address when a page loads.

How long we keep it

Sign-in links expire after 15 minutes and work once. Sessions end after 30 days or when you sign out. Everything else stays until you delete your account.

Deleting it

Go to your ACCOUNT page and press DELETE ACCOUNT. Straight away we remove your email, name, profile, home skin, wardrobe, MAKER® settings, GOOD® COINS, GOOD® CRATE pulls, achievements and your own trade history, and anything you have listed on MARKET® comes down. In other players' trade histories and in the suggestion box your name is replaced with DELETED.

Comments you've already posted on STUDY® or on profiles stay up under your name; to have them taken down, ask us. MINECRAFT STAND codes you've made keep working: the signed skin stays with us, MineSkin and Minecraft. Ask us to remove them.

Younger players

If you're under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before making an account. Parents can ask us to delete a child's account at any time.
