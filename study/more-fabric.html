MORE FABRIC

GOOD® FRIDAY PROGRAMME®: COFFEE/MATCHA.

By GOOD® November 20, 2026

Cozy has been a much bigger part of fashion than we give it credit for.

In the early 2010s, beauty was pain. Skinny was in: skinny jeans, skinny shirts, Rick Owens and his skinny tees. Fashion then was restrictive, and honestly, it wasn’t very welcoming.

Now the early 2000s are coming back in so many ways. JNCO jeans are back. People are wearing baggy denim again. And more fabric means more opportunity. When I think of cozy, I don’t think of fashion giving up. I think of a bigger canvas to make something interesting on.

More fabric means more opportunity.

It’s nearly Thanksgiving, then Christmas, and I’m starting to feel the warmth these two months bring: being with family, being around the holidays. The older I get, the more it feels like the best time of the year. Life stops for a bit. I’m lucky enough to be able to step away and spend that time with family and friends, and as you get older, it’s a time you learn to cherish.

So this Friday, COFFEE and MATCHA lead the FRIDAY PROGRAMME®. I hope everyone enjoys it. It’s a small taste of what’s coming.
