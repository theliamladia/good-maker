Nº01

What we do

Skin design

Minecraft skins built pixel by pixel, in Classic and Slim, with both layers working: hoods that stand off the head, hems that drape over shoes, cuffs that sit off the arm.

Capsules and collections

A whole drop, not a one-off: the pieces, the colourways, the names and the story that ties them together. AMONG THE TREES® went from twelve sketches to a live capsule.

Colour and naming

Colour systems with names people remember. Every GOOD® colour has a name, a hex and a reason, from GOOD® BLUE to CHOCOLATE™.

Identity at pixel scale

Marks and logos that survive at 4×4 pixels. We redrew our own G three times to get it right, and wrote about why.

Renders and lookbooks

3D renders, turnarounds, ghost mannequins and lookbook sheets on real players, ready for a launch post or a pitch deck.

Tools and pages

Interactive pages that put the work in people's hands, like THE MAKER, where anyone can try every piece on their own skin.

Nº02

Selected work

CAPSULE · AMONG THE TREES®

Twelve pieces, three colourways, one season.

GOOD® CAPSULE Nº2: six looks for GOOD® and BABY®, designed, named, rendered on the crew and launched in THE MAKER with a landing page of its own.

PRODUCT · JEAN®

The best jean in Minecraft.

Six washes, four cuts, two layers. Rips that read at a glance, hems that stack, a sagging fit with boxers that peek out. Then a long read in STUDY® on how it was made.

FOOTWEAR · CHELSEA®, WORKBOOT®, DUCK BOOT®

Product Research: Boots

Footwear drawn under every bottom in the catalogue. The jean breaks over the boot in front and drapes to the heel at the back, the way trousers really sit.

IDENTITY · THE G

Old G to new G.

Three generations of one letter, each redrawn for a 64 by 64 skin: narrow, then square, then open. Quiet enough to sit off to the side, strong enough to own a chest.

THE CREW

How it works

  1. 01

    Brief

    Tell us on Discord what you need: a single skin, a collection, a colour system, a launch.

  2. 02

    Drafts

    Several directions, rendered on real players, side by side so choosing is easy.

  3. 03

    Refine

    Turnarounds, close-ups and small changes until every pixel is right.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Final files, renders and names, ready to wear and ready to post.
